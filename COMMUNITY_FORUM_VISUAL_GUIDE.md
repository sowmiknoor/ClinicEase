## 🔄 User Interactions

### Hover Effects
- **Cards**: Lift up with shadow
- **Buttons**: Darker background
- **Pills**: Border color change
- **Links**: Underline appears

### Click Actions
- **Medicine Card**: Open detail modal
- **Health Tip Card**: Expand in place
- **Research Paper**: Open detail modal
- **Search**: Real-time filtering
- **Category Pill**: Filter instantly

### Animations
- **Page Load**: Fade in
- **Card Hover**: Translate Y (-4px)
- **Modal Open**: Scale + fade
- **Filter**: Smooth transition
